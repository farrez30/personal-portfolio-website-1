Inspiration
  Discography by Dmytro Barylo (github.com/dmtrbrl/discography): the idea of a rack of
  jewel cases with a booklet and a player. Rebuilt from scratch; no code, texture or
  font from that project is used.
  Codrops Glass Xylophone: the idea behind the contact helix.
  Meng To, build-threejs-scroll-worlds skill (github.com/MengTo/Skills, MIT): the idea of one
  persistent 3D world with a chapter ledger driven by native scroll. Our code is our own.
