# English titles for story v5

21 Nov 2026. Decision of 20 Nov: the EN titles are written on their own (not translated) and Farrez
picks from 2 to 3 options per title before they are final. Same rules as the ID set: the subject is
the result or the problem, short and calm, at most one "X, not Y" in the whole run, no technical
terms, each title answers the reader's worry in the left column. **(R)** = recommended, currently
in `content/cerita-v5.js` until Farrez chooses.

| # | Worry | A | B | C | Why the recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 dorongan | "Every night the numbers come out different." | **Every night's tally comes out different.** (R) | The nightly count never matches. | Closing up takes longer than selling. | A keeps the concrete nightly moment; B is close but "never" overclaims; C changes the worry |
| 02 harga-diam | "A new system is expensive, I'll leave it." | **Leaving it alone costs money too.** (R) | Doing nothing has a price. | Overtime and missing stock get paid too. | A is spoken and plain; B sounds like a proverb; C carries two ideas |
| 03 tarikan | "What does it look like once it's in place?" | **Open your phone, today's numbers are there.** (R) | Today's numbers, on one screen. | Picture the morning after. | A is a moment and an object; B drops the person; C fails "so what?" |
| 04 biaya | "The cost will balloon halfway." | **You know the price before we start.** (R) | The price is clear from day one. | New request, new phase, its own price. | A puts the reader first and names the moment of signing; B is equal; C needs the picture |
| 05 kelangsungan | "If the vendor disappears, what happens to my system?" | **It keeps running, whoever looks after it.** (R) | Still running if someone else takes over. | What you get is written down from the start. | A answers the worry head on; B is longer; C is right but abstract |
| 06 data | "If it's hacked or the server breaks, is the data gone?" | **Broken in one place, whole in another.** (R) | Safe, and it can be restored. | One copy fails, the other holds. | A mirrors the ID line and is concrete; B is abstract; C is close but less rhythmic |
