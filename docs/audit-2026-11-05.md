## Findings

- **[P1, fixed] Header wraps on phones.** At 375 px the bar was 118 px tall and the sticky rack
  (top 60 px) slid under it, hiding "Tentang" and "Kontak". Below 480 px the bar now stays on one
  row: the name shortens to FARREZ (the link keeps the full name as its label) and only the other
  language is shown. Verified: bar 61 px, page width 375 px, no horizontal scroll.
- **[P2, fixed] Rack arrows 25 × 44 px.** `.panggung__sudut` now has `min-width: 44px`.
- **[P3, open] Mode switch options 36 px tall.** Above the AA minimum, below the 44 px comfort
  target. Raising it means a taller header.
- **[P3, open] Small mono text.** Meta lines use 11 to 12 px (`0.6875rem`, `0.75rem`) in the vinyl
  player, rack captions and hologram chips. Readable on desktop; check on a real phone.
- **[Note] Contrast scan.** The automated scan flagged the headline's second half and the rack's
  corner links. Both are false positives: the headline fades in with scroll by design (the text is
  in the DOM), and the corner links are dark ink `#22201d` on the beige stage gradient, which the
  scan read as the dark page background.

## Positive
